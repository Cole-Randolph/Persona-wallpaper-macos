# Persona-wallpaper-macos
Modular Persona 5 Royal wallpaper engine that creates layered, time-based scenes with characters and subtle ambient effects. Generates static PNGs and plans HEIC dynamic wallpapers for macOS. Built with SwiftUI and JSON configs for easy customization and expansion. Perfect for fans who want dynamic desktop art.

##🎭 Persona 5 Dynamic Wallpaper Engine

Welcome to a world where your desktop becomes a living canvas inspired by Persona 5 Royal.

Picture this: layered scenes of your favorite Tokyo hangouts, characters poised in their roles, and subtle touches—like steam curling from a coffee cup or soft lights flickering—that bring still moments quietly alive.

This app is your gateway to crafting these immersive, time-aware wallpapers that shift with the day—morning, afternoon, and night—transforming your screen into a personal stage for the Phantom Thieves.

Built around modular assets and flexible configs, it’s designed for creators who love control and storytelling. While I’m building the app myself, this repo is a testament to that vision—a starting point for anyone who shares the passion.

Step in, imagine, and create your own dynamic Persona 5 scenes.
